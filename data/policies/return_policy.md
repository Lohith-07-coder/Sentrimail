# Return Policy

## 1. General Returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of receipt. Items must be in original condition.

## 2. Refunds
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

## 3. Exceptions
Software and digital downloads are not eligible for returns.

## 4. Damaged Items
If an item arrives damaged, customers must report it within 48 hours for a full replacement.
